## Abstract

The Thresholded Circulatory Ledger researches a liquidity protocol with a physical cash substrate and zero-knowledge redemption.
The executable prototype combines a fixed-supply ledger, thresholded demurrage, a redistribution vault, backed note escrow, and a Poseidon-based secret-knowledge proof.
It does not yet implement shielded payments, physical enrollment, or per-person immunity enforcement.
The normative executable specification is `05_protocol.md`.
